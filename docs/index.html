Yeh app kya hai

App aur website, dono ki security check

Sentinel ek AI security app hai jo aapke phone par chalta hai. Koi bhi Android app ya website link do — ye AI se padh-check karke aasaan Hinglish me batata hai ki usme koi khatra hai ya nahi: malware, data-chori, scam ya phishing. Aapko technical kuch samajhna nahi padta.

📱 App Scan

Phone se koi bhi app chuno (.apk, .apks, .xapk — sab support). Sentinel us app ko andar tak padhta hai aur batata hai ki koi chhupi hui data-chori, backdoor ya khatarnak code to nahi. Sirf permission dekhke andaza nahi lagata — asli code check karta hai.

🌐 Web Scan

Koi bhi link daalo (kisi bhi type ka). Sentinel website ko asal browser me kholtA hai aur dekhta hai ki wo fake login, phishing ya scam to nahi, aapka password/OTP chura to nahi raha. "Free diamond/topup" jaise scams pakad leta hai.

Ye zaruri kyu hai

Scam apps aur phishing links se bachein

Aajkal WhatsApp/Telegram par fake "free reward", "free diamond", aur fake bank/Paytm login links bahut tezi se fail rahe hain. Pata lagana mushkil hai ki kisi app par bharosa karna safe hai ya nahi. Sentinel AI se turant bata deta hai — taaki aapka paisa, data aur accounts safe rahein.

  • Free-fire/UC/diamond jaise "free reward" scams turant pehchanta hai.
  • Fake PayPal/Instagram/bank login pages (phishing) pakad leta hai.
  • App me chhupi IMEI/contacts/SMS chori aur backdoor dhoondhta hai.
  • Saara check aapke phone par — scan ke baad temp files apne aap delete.
